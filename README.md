# AI Journey

> *"The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step."* - Lao Tzu

Welcome to my AI learning journey! This repository is a living chronicle of my exploration through the fascinating world of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science.

## Why This Journey?

Every concept mastered, every bug debugged, and every model trained is a step forward. This isn't just a collection of notes—it's a map of growth, documenting the challenges faced, solutions discovered, and insights gained along the way.

Whether you're a fellow learner searching for resources, a future version of myself revisiting fundamentals, or someone just starting out, I hope these materials serve as helpful guideposts on your own journey.

This repository captures hands-on work, notes, and projects across multiple domains:

### Applied Learning

### Specialized Topics

### Resources

- **Wisconsin Data Science** (`wq-datascience`) - Additional coursework
